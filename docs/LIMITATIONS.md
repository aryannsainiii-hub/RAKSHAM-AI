# Limitations and responsible use

- Constant-velocity illustration is not orbital propagation and omits gravity and perturbations.
- Synthetic coordinates are not observations. They do not represent real satellites or warnings.
- Geometric distance bands are heuristic. No collision probability or covariance is calculated.
- Candidate Δv is illustrative and does not establish safety or flight readiness.
- The frontend orbit graphic is a schematic, not a coordinate-accurate visualization.
- CelesTrak ingestion, SGP4/OMM, PostgreSQL, Alembic, authentication, rate limiting, background jobs, and operational deployment safeguards are not implemented.
- Object catalogue size and coverage are only the tiny built-in fixture; results must not be interpreted as complete screening.
- AI is not connected. The explanation fallback summarizes limitations and does not author calculations.

Use only for research/education. Validate data provenance, epochs, frames, uncertainty and results independently before any operational interpretation.
